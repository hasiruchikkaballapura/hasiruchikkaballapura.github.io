Privacy policy

School adoption app · last updated 5 November 2026

The Hasiru Shaale school adoption app is used by registered volunteers, coordinators and office staff of Isha Foundation's Hasiru Shaale programme (Chikkaballapura) to follow up on the schools they adopt.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name and email address, only to check that you are a registered volunteer. We do not see or store your Google password.
  • What you enter in the app: school visit and call records, school location (taken from your phone only when you tap the button), photos, audio or video you choose to upload, and corrections to school details.

How it is used and stored

  • The information is used only to run the Hasiru Shaale programme: assigning schools, following up on saplings and seeds, and planning support for schools.
  • It is stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive belonging to the programme, and is seen only by the programme's office, coordinators and admins.
  • It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone outside the programme.
  • A half-filled form may be kept on your own phone until you save or discard it.

Contact

For questions about this app, write to: Hasiru Shaale, Chikkaballapura · hasiru.chikkaballapura@sadhguru.org
